ARTIST BIO

I am more interested in expressing “feelings” than explaining emotions.

Chang Shu is an illustrator and multidisciplinary artist whose practice moves between illustration, digital media, interaction and imagined worlds.

Her work explores imagination, dizziness, hallucination, symbolism and the unstable boundary between reality and constructed experience.

Born in China, Chang developed an early fascination with literature, philosophy, classical music and visual culture. These interests continue to shape the way she constructs images, systems and fictional spaces.

Rather than describing everything directly, she is interested in images and experiences that can be sensed before they can be completely explained.

When she is not making work, she is probably reading, playing games, thinking about vampires, or trying not to leave the house.
