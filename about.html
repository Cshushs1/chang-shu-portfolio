02 / BIO

Chang Shu is an illustrator and multidisciplinary creative whose practice moves between illustration, digital media, interaction and imagined worlds.

Her illustrations often construct stories through imagination, dizziness, hallucination and metaphor. Rather than explaining everything immediately, the images invite careful observation and interpretation.

Born in China, Chang developed an early interest in philosophy, literature, classical music and visual culture. She is often more interested in expressing a feeling than describing an emotion directly.

When she is not making work, she is probably reading, playing games, thinking about vampires, or trying not to leave the house.
